Institutional Hierarchy

Democratic Governance with Centralized Accountability

The administrative machinery of PM SHRI Telangana Government Model School (TGMS), Somaram operates in accordance with the regulatory standards established by the Department of School Education, Government of Telangana, and the Model Schools Society.

The Principal serves as the administrative and academic executive, supported by subject panels of Post Graduate Teachers (PGT), Trained Graduate Teachers (TGT), the School Management Committee (SMC), and the Parent-Teacher Association (PTA).

  • Transparent administrative processes with regular record audits.
  • Direct oversight of student attendance, teacher lesson plans, and laboratory safety.
  • Bi-monthly School Management Committee meetings with community representatives.
  • Student Cabinet empowering pupils with early governance and democratic responsibility.

Committees & Councils

Functional Administrative Bodies

Distinct committees ensuring high academic standards, foolproof safety, and active community involvement at TGMS Somaram.

🏛️

School Management Committee (SMC)

Composed of the Principal, parent members, elected local representatives, and educationists. Responsible for campus development, infrastructure oversight, and school welfare.

📊

Academic Advisory Council

Led by senior PGT faculty to supervise CCE evaluation schedules, formative and summative test design, syllabus pacing, and the "Alchemy" remedial mentorship initiative.

🛡️

Discipline & Safety Committee

Tasked with campus discipline, periodic bag inspections, hygiene protocols, student safety drills, and prompt redressal of grievances.

🤝

Parent-Teacher Association (PTA)

Facilitates regular constructive dialogue between guardians and teaching faculty to review child progress, attendance consistency, and emotional well-being.

🎓

Examination & Evaluation Board

Administers SSC and Intermediate board examination preparation, internal assessments, mock tests, and comprehensive result analyses.

⭐

Student Cabinet & House Council

Includes Head Boy, Head Girl, Raman House Captain, Bhaskara House Captain, and Sports Captain, fostering student voice and leadership.

Campus Standards

Code of Conduct & Institutional Rules

Essential standards expected of every member of the TGMS Somaram community.

Student Responsibilities

  • Strict adherence to the official school uniform, black polished shoes, and neat grooming.
  • Minimum attendance of 75% is mandatory for appearing in annual board examinations.
  • Mobile phones, electronic gadgets, and objectionable materials are strictly prohibited.
  • Respect towards faculty members, school property, library books, and lab instruments.
  • Active participation in house activities, sports meets, and national day observances.

Parental Partnership

  • Regularly check student school diaries, progress cards, and homework notebooks.
  • Attend all scheduled Parent-Teacher Association (PTA) meetings.
  • Provide written leave applications with medical certificates for student absences.
  • Encourage daily reading habits and minimum 2 hours of self-study at home.
  • Notify the administration immediately regarding any change in contact phone number or address.
