About Our School

Striving Continuously for Academic & Co-Scholastic Excellence

Telangana Government Model School (TGMS), Somaram (PM SHRI School, Saidapur Mandal) was established to provide premier, qualitative English-medium secondary and higher secondary education to talented students from rural and semi-urban communities.

Our core mission is to integrate all modern developments in the educational field while catering to fulfill all physical, psychological, and emotional needs of our students. We groom multi-dimensional personalities equipped to face real-world challenges and emerge as conscientious citizens.

  • Holistic curriculum designed for Classes 6 through 10 (Secondary) and Intermediate (MPC, BiPC, CEC, MEC).
  • Inculcating an inbuilt sense of patriotism, national integration, and universal brotherhood.
  • Continuous Comprehensive Evaluation (CCE) with targeted diagnostic and remedial coaching.
  • Co-scholastic programs including Mock Assemblies, Science Congress, and Youth Parliaments.

Campus Facilities & Features

What Makes TGMS Somaram Special

A pace-setting institution combining modern PM SHRI infrastructure with compassionate student mentoring.

🔬

Advanced Science Labs

Fully equipped laboratories for Physics, Chemistry, and Biological Sciences allowing students to conduct experiments and engage in hands-on inquiry.

💻

Digital ICT Computer Lab

Modern computer laboratory with high-speed internet and digital learning tools to empower students with 21st-century technological literacy.

📚

Rich Library & Resources

Extensive repository of textbooks, reference volumes, periodicals, newspapers, and encyclopedias fostering a continuous reading habit.

⚽

Sports & Physical Fitness

Spacious playground supporting athletics, volleyball, cricket, kho-kho, and yoga, instilling sportsmanship, physical endurance, and discipline.

🎯

"Alchemy" Mentorship

Specialized institutional program identifying academic gaps early and providing one-on-one remedial support to transform non-performing students into achievers.

🏛️

House System & Leadership

Students divided into Raman House and Bhaskara House to encourage healthy inter-house competitions, mock elections, and cabinet leadership.
