Message from the Principal

Fostering Knowledge, Integrity & Excellence

"Knowledge is treasure, but practice is its key. At TGMS Somaram, our fundamental objective is to provide knowledge-based practice at all times, nurturing each child into a capable, responsible, and self-reliant human being."

Welcome to the official portal of Telangana Government Model School (TGMS), Somaram. As a designated PM SHRI Model School, our institution stands as a beacon of academic opportunity for boys and girls from rural and semi-urban communities across Saidapur Mandal and Karimnagar District. Our educational philosophy transcends traditional rote memorization. We believe in preparing young minds to think critically, communicate articulately, and face real-world challenges with self-confidence.

With modern laboratories, e-learning classrooms, sports grounds, and a dedicated team of Post Graduate (PGT) and Trained Graduate (TGT) educators, we strive continuously for academic excellence. We place equal emphasis on physical health, psychological well-being, moral integrity, patriotism, and universal brotherhood.

I urge all parents, teachers, and well-wishers to work hand-in-hand to cultivate an environment where our students can unleash their full potential.

With warm regards,
K. B. Dharma Prakash

Administrative Excellence

Duties & Responsibilities of Class / Co-Class Teachers

A model school is built upon systematic discipline, proactive student care, and an unwavering commitment to learning. As published in our institutional charter, class teachers and co-class teachers uphold specific responsibilities to ensure the highest standards of safety, hygiene, and academic growth.

Our leadership framework guarantees that no student is left behind, whether they need extra tutoring in science and mathematics or encouragement in sports and cultural arts.

Administrative Office & Academic Monitoring Desk
Official Institutional Guidelines

Class Teacher Code of Operations

Documented guidelines governing student discipline, safety, attendance, and welfare.

⭐ Class Discipline & Student Leadership

  • Select and groom responsible class monitors to assist in maintaining discipline and effective control in the teacher's absence.
  • Train monitors in conflict resolution, respectful behavior, and queue management during assemblies and recess.
  • Ensure class decorum during transitions between periods and laboratory sessions.

💧 Environmental & Resource Conservation

  • Instruct the class regularly on conserving precious water, electricity, and paper.
  • Ensure fans, tube lights, and computer workstations are strictly switched off when leaving classrooms or labs.
  • Inculcate zero-waste habits and cleanliness in the school environment (Swachh Vidyalaya).

👔 Personal Hygiene & Grooming Checks

  • Check the class routinely during morning assembly for clean and proper uniforms, polished shoes, trimmed hair, and clean nails.
  • Encourage proper personal hygiene and healthy hand-washing habits before mid-day meals.
  • Promote cleanliness consciousness as an integral part of individual character.

📈 Academic Support & Morale Boosting

  • Boost the morale of slow learners, non-performing students, and underachievers through affirmative encouragement.
  • Regularly communicate with subject teachers (PGTs & TGTs) regarding any learning bottlenecks or subject-specific issues.
  • Enroll struggling students in the institutional "Alchemy" remedial mentorship program.

🛡️ Student Safety & Bag Inspections

  • Check students' school bags at random intervals on a regular basis for prohibited or contraband items.
  • Instill safety consciousness among students regarding seasonal health, emergency evacuation, and lab precautions.
  • Ensure students strictly follow campus boundaries and road safety rules during arrival and departure.

📋 Attendance & Parent Communication

  • Monitor the attendance register daily, marking absentees accurately and cautioning chronic defaulters.
  • Maintain an updated register with parents' phone numbers, residential addresses, and emergency contacts.
  • Communicate with parents regularly regarding attendance, behavioral concerns, and academic report cards; track leave applications and medical certificates.

🏫 Classroom Maintenance & Infrastructure

  • Ensure safety, neatness, and proper arrangement of desks, benches, display boards, blackboards, dusters, cupboards, and dustbins through monitors.
  • Inspect and report damage to classroom window panes, electrical switchboards, fans, tube lights, and doors for immediate repair.
  • Encourage students to create vibrant educational charts for class bulletin boards.

🤝 Rapport, Trust & Principal Reporting

  • Keep the Principal informed immediately regarding any disciplinary incidents, medical emergencies, or grievances.
  • Build an open, trusting rapport with all students, remaining accessible to them during times of academic or emotional need.
  • Instill an inbuilt sense of belonging to the school, state, and nation.
